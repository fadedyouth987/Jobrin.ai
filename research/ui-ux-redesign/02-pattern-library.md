# Jobrin.ai Pattern Library

## Research synthesis

The strongest field-service products organize the product around a job record, a dispatch surface and a short mobile close-out flow. The strongest CRM products add fast list/board switching, record previews and an attention-first inbox. The reference SaaS products add precision: predictable spacing, dense but configurable tables, obvious system state, keyboard access and restrained motion.

Jobrin should combine those ideas around one rule: each screen answers “what needs attention, what is the record, and what is the next safe action?” It should avoid enterprise configuration density until the underlying workflow is complete.

## Direct competitor workflow matrix

This matrix records the recurring structural choices visible in current official product/help material. “Not verified” means the reviewed public source did not establish the behavior; it is not a claim that the product lacks it.

| Product | Navigation / information architecture | Lists and record detail | Scheduling and mobile field work | Quotes, invoices, portal and feedback states |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jobber | Client, request, quote, job/visit and invoice are connected stages; creation is available from global and record context.[1] | Jobs list combines filters/actions with workload metrics; job records link the commercial chain.[1] | Day/week/month schedule can add unscheduled panels and map; completed visits de-emphasise visually.[2] | Quote preview/approval and invoice payment live in a mobile-friendly passwordless Client Hub; deposits and payment schedules carry forward.[1][3] |
| ServiceTitan | Office dispatch, field execution, customer and finance modules are one end-to-end system.[4] | Rich history keeps property, customer, job, membership, invoice and audit context together.[4] | Dispatch board uses capacity, skills, route and real-time status; mobile supports offline work, forms, photos, estimates, signatures and payments.[4][5] | Estimates can be built/presented on site and completed with signature/payment; detailed failure-state UX was not verifiable publicly.[5] |
| Housecall Pro | Global Create, customer intake, job detail and Schedule can all start the same job/estimate flow.[6][7] | Customisable job/estimate lists support search, filters and columns; job detail holds customer, schedule, dispatch, items, notes, checklists and files.[7] | Calendar exposes unscheduled work and creates a job from a selected slot; mobile includes recurrence, arrival window, assignment and notification choice.[6] | Estimates can stay attached to an existing job to avoid duplicate schedule records; portal detail was not verified in the reviewed source.[7] |
| Simpro | IA follows estimating, scheduling, execution, costing, invoice/payment and reporting, with separate field modules.[8] | Job context includes site history, notes, attachments, labour, materials, contractors and equipment.[8] | Day/week/month staff schedule; mobile day schedule, clock on/off, travel time, photos, audits, assets, signatures and offline-oriented field access.[8][9] | Mobile can create/present quotes, invoice and collect payment; customer portal supports online invoice payment.[8][9] |
| Fergus | Job Card is the centre; left tools and tabs expose scheduling, labour, stock, supplier documents, finance, notes/history and files.[10] | One job can have phases, cost/profit context and timestamped history.[10] | Dashboard moves jobs into Scheduling; assignment and timed scheduling are separate; drag/drop works from an unscheduled list into staff calendar.[11] | Quote versions, publish state, progress invoices and card payment are explicit; customer reminders use configurable SMS/email timing.[10][11] |
| Tradify | Mobile bottom navigation exposes dashboard, jobs, quotes, invoices, scheduler and map; jobs group into Today/New/Active/All.[12] | Job dashboard links customer, original enquiry, quotes, invoices, time and contact/location actions.[12] | Day/week staff scheduler resembles desktop; job timer and timesheet entries are available within job context.[12] | Mobile creates/edits invoices with notes, photos, videos and signatures; detailed portal behavior was not verified.[12] |
| Service Fusion | IA centres on customer → estimate → schedule/dispatch → work order → invoice/payment.[13] | Customer and project context are part of one contractor workflow; public source does not expose precise table density.[13] | Technician mobile supports schedules, service requests, job acceptance, customer info, close-out, invoices and payments, including offline positioning.[13] | Estimate options, e-signature, portal and recurring/progress billing vary by package; state detail was not publicly verifiable.[13] |
| FieldPulse | Modules separate office, field and customer experiences while sharing customer/job data.[14] | Customer profiles can consolidate multiple sites/contacts and preserve service history.[15] | Dispatch board uses technician lanes/status cards; routing/map and custom workflows connect to mobile work orders.[14] | Estimate preview uses Send/Present; customer portal exposes jobs/amounts/status, booking, proposal approval and invoice payment.[14] |
| Workiz | Schedule, CRM, Message Center, phone, jobs, estimates, invoices, payments, marketing and automations are integrated.[16] | CRM shows active opportunity, client details, prior jobs/files and overdue payments together.[16] | Drag/drop schedule filters by expertise/team; mobile exposes jobs/leads/tasks, ETA, status, call/message, payment and time logging.[16][17] | Estimates copy to job/invoice; invoice dashboard distinguishes no amount/due/overdue/paid; client portal carries document/payment links.[16][17] |
| GorillaDesk | Features are explicitly grouped by owner, admin and technician jobs.[18] | CRM and reporting exist, but exact record layout/table density were not verifiable in the reviewed public page.[18] | Drag/drop calendar, scheduling/dispatch, GPS and route optimisation support field coordination; mobile is the technician surface.[18] | Customer portal covers estimates, appointments and invoice payment; invoicing, review generation and e-sign documents share the platform.[18] |
| BigChange | CRM, schedule, mobile, finance, fleet and reports form one operational suite.[19] | Each job carries customer/site/equipment history and proof-of-service information.[19] | Drag/drop updates engineer devices; scheduling checks capacity/skills/equipment and optimises routes; mobile captures status, photos and signatures.[19] | Portal supports booking, progress and job cards; automated confirmations/ETAs/reminders carry state to customers.[19] |
| Zuper | Work orders connect customers, properties, assets, contracts, estimates, invoices, parts, routes and activity.[20][21] | Complete status timeline, history, activity and related objects are available inside the work order.[20] | Mobile prioritises assigned work, checklists, media, chat, time/location and offline operation; office uses qualification/proximity for dispatch.[20][21] | Estimates, digital payments and customer portal are part of capability tiers; detailed quote-builder and empty/error behavior were not verifiable publicly.[21] |

## Product-by-product structural notes

| Product | What works and why | Translation to Jobrin | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jobber | Jobs connect clients, quotes, visits and invoices; the schedule adds day/week/month views plus unscheduled work, and Client Hub lets customers approve, view visits and pay without a conventional password.[1][2][3] | Use a job-centred detail view, unscheduled rail on Schedule and secure customer links that eventually converge into a portal. | Do not copy feature breadth before Jobrin has pagination, capacity and payout correctness. |
| ServiceTitan | The dispatch board combines technician capacity, skills, route context and live changes; mobile carries customer history, estimates, forms, signatures and payments to the technician.[4][5] | Add staff lanes, conflict/capacity cues and a field mode that opens on today’s assigned work. | Avoid its enterprise-scale configuration density and revenue-optimisation language for small crews. |
| Housecall Pro | A job can be scheduled from creation, detail or calendar; the calendar includes unscheduled items, and job records combine customer, dispatch, line items, notes, checklists and attachments.[6][7] | Let create flows inherit the selected calendar slot and allow the same action from list, customer and schedule context. | Do not surface every job property in the first mobile viewport. |
| Simpro | Day/week/month scheduling, job costing and mobile clock-on/off, notes, photos, compliance, signatures, invoices and payments form one continuous field workflow.[8][9] | Make cost-to-price visibility and field completion first-class within Job Detail. | Avoid desktop ERP density and inventory complexity until the data model is ready. |
| Fergus | The Job Card is the central workspace with scheduling, labour, supplier documents, stock, financial summary, notes/history and files; scheduling distinguishes assignment from a timed visit.[10][11] | Use a stable job header with task-oriented tabs and show “assigned” separately from “scheduled.” | Do not reproduce deep left-tool nesting inside Jobrin’s already deep global sidebar. |
| Tradify | Mobile groups jobs into Today/New/Active/All and keeps direct call, map, timer, quote and invoice actions close to the job.[12] | Use role-aware mobile tabs and a sticky field action bar for call, directions, timer and status. | Do not make mobile a compressed desktop table. |
| Service Fusion | The core platform is a linear estimate → schedule → dispatch → invoice journey, with a mobile app for customer context, work orders, invoices and payments.[13] | Keep the revenue lifecycle visible and make the next transition explicit. | Avoid generic “all in one” dashboard tiles that do not lead to action. |
| FieldPulse | Customer sites, service history, booking/portal flows and estimate/invoice acceptance are shown as connected surfaces; dispatch uses status-coloured work cards.[14][15] | Add customer-site context to records and keep customer approval/payment screens clean and branded. | Do not add multi-site UI until Jobrin exposes corresponding customer-address maintenance. |
| Workiz | Communication, AI call context, CRM, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, booking and automations are connected; mobile can update status, call, message and collect payment.[16][17] | Connect Inbox conversations to customer/job creation and show the source conversation on the resulting record. | Avoid promising autonomous dispatch while Jobrin’s capacity and provider setup remain incomplete. |
| GorillaDesk | Role-oriented feature grouping separates owner, admin and technician needs; portal, route optimisation and dispatch stay close to jobs.[18] | Drive navigation and default views from the user’s role rather than showing one long universal menu. | Avoid marketing-style feature groupings inside the working app. |
| BigChange | Drag/drop scheduling updates mobile workers immediately; job records carry customer history, site notes and equipment details, with offline proof-of-service patterns.[19] | Add real-time schedule feedback, offline-safe field drafts and proof-of-service capture. | Avoid fleet telemetry and route features until precise-location policies and backend support exist. |
| Zuper | Mobile work orders expose history, assets, parts, activity, chat and offline mode; office scheduling considers qualification and proximity.[20][21] | Treat offline resilience as a requirement for job execution, with sync state on each pending field action. | Avoid highly configurable workflows that make the small-business setup harder. |
| HubSpot | Record pages use properties at left, an activity/overview centre and associations at right, keeping history and related objects visible.[22] | Use a three-region desktop customer/job detail that collapses to ordered sections on mobile. | Avoid dozens of cards and custom tabs before core record maintenance is complete. |
| Attio | Tables and kanban are views over the same records; a resizable right-side preview preserves list context, supports next/previous navigation and shows highlights, activity and tasks.[23][24] | Add list/board view toggles and a right-side record preview for customers, leads, jobs and financial lists. | Avoid arbitrary schema customisation until Jobrin’s fixed trade workflow is dependable. |
| Close | Inbox combines inbound communication, tasks and opportunity reminders; each item is actionable and shows quick lead context. Pipeline guidance suggests a concrete next step.[25][26] | Merge urgent conversations, overdue documents, approvals and stalled leads into a Command Centre attention queue. | Avoid sales-team language and productivity pressure that does not fit field-service work. |
| Pipedrive | Pipeline cards expose value, contact, owner and next activity; sorting defaults to next activity and stale deals can be highlighted.[27] | Add next-action and age cues to Jobrin lead cards; make overdue follow-up visible. | Avoid unlimited custom pipelines; keep the real Jobrin lifecycle understandable. |
| Linear | Priority Inbox separates action from noise; list/board views share behavior; search, peek and keyboard shortcuts reduce navigation without clutter.[28][29][30] | Add an attention filter, consistent keyboard shortcuts and a non-destructive preview layer. | Do not hide essential field actions behind keyboard-only controls. |
| Vercel | Chronological activity logs record actor, event, target and exact time; runtime logs are filterable and grouped around a request.[31][32] | Give automations, integrations and AI actions a searchable event stream with clear correlation IDs and record links. | Avoid developer terminology on owner-facing pages. |
| Stripe | Stable list/detail URL structure across payments, customers, invoices and subscriptions creates predictable financial navigation.[33] | Give quotes, invoices and payments dedicated detail routes with consistent financial summary, timeline and actions. | Do not expose raw provider objects or internal payment IDs as the primary UI. |
| Notion | One dataset can have table, board, calendar, list and timeline views; records open in side peek by default so context is retained.[34][35] | Offer only the views that suit each Jobrin dataset: table/board for leads, list/calendar for jobs, table for financials. | Avoid open-ended page building and view proliferation for trade users. |
| Ramp | Transaction workflows emphasise pending requirements, needs review, ready to sync and role-limited views; row data supports filters and bulk work.[36][37] | Make payment/invoice exceptions explicit and show what blocks reconciliation or payout. | Avoid card/spend-policy concepts unrelated to receivables. |
| Mercury | Financial tables drive their charts; filters, grouping, saved views, inline categorisation and bulk editing keep analysis and action aligned.[38] | Make report charts respond to the same date/source/status filters as the underlying table. | Avoid banking-specific controls and excessive charting on daily operations pages. |

## Adoptable named patterns

| Pattern | Source | Jobrin destination | Specification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attention Queue** | Linear Priority Inbox; Close Inbox[25][28] | `/app`, `/app/inbox`, `/app/notifications`, `/app/approvals` | One ranked queue for missed/new conversations, bookings needing confirmation, stalled quotes, overdue invoices, failed runs and pending approvals. Every row has one primary action and a reason it appears. |
| **Record Preview Rail** | Attio record preview; Notion side peek[23][35] | Customer, lead, job, quote, invoice and payment lists | Open a 420–560 px resizable rail on desktop while retaining the list; full-screen sheet on mobile. Arrow navigation moves through current filtered results. |
| **Job Command Header** | Fergus Job Card; ServiceTitan mobile[5][10] | `/app/jobs/:id` | Persistent number/title/status/customer/schedule header. Desktop actions at right; mobile sticky actions: Call, Directions, Start/Stop, Status, More. |
| **Assigned vs Scheduled** | Fergus scheduling[11] | Schedule and Job Detail | Display assignment as a person/resource and schedule as a time block. Never imply one from the other. |
| **Unscheduled Work Rail** | Jobber and Housecall Pro schedules[2][6] | `/app/schedule` | Collapsible rail of unscheduled jobs ordered by priority/age; dragging proposes a slot and assignee, then saves after conflict validation. |
| **Field Day List** | Tradify and Simpro mobile[9][12] | Mobile `/app`, `/app/schedule`, `/app/jobs` | Default to Today with large time, customer, suburb, status and next action. Provide New/Active/All as secondary tabs. |
| **Field Close-out Checklist** | Simpro, BigChange and Zuper[9][19][20] | `/app/jobs/:id` | Ordered checklist: time stopped, required checklist complete, photos attached, materials reviewed, signature captured, status complete. Resume safely after lost signal. |
| **Quote-to-Cash Continuity** | Jobber, Fergus and Workiz[1][10][17] | Quote builder, Job Detail, Invoice Detail | Approved scope copies forward with visible provenance. Show quote → job → invoice relationships and prevent duplicate conversion. |
| **Good/Better/Best Options** | Workiz proposals; ServiceTitan mobile estimates[5][16] | Future quote builder | Optional grouped quote choices only after the quote model supports alternatives. ⚠️ BACKEND GAP. |
| **Client Self-service Hub** | Jobber Client Hub; FieldPulse portal[3][14] | Customer-facing routes | Passwordless secure access to appointments, quotes, invoices, receipts and work requests with clear expiry/revocation. ⚠️ BACKEND GAP beyond token/session foundation. |
| **Views over One Dataset** | Attio, Linear and Notion[24][29][34] | Leads, jobs, schedule and reports | Table/board/calendar views share filters and selection. Do not duplicate records or maintain separate state models. |
| **Next Activity on Card** | Pipedrive and Close[25][27] | `/app/leads` | Lead cards show customer, value, age, next activity and last contact. Stalled state is time-based and explains the threshold. |
| **Financial Exception States** | Ramp and Mercury[36][38] | Invoices, payments, reports | Separate Draft, Needs delivery, Due, Overdue, Part paid, Payment processing, Paid, Failed and Payout pending. Filters drive both totals and rows. |
| **Chronological Audit Stream** | Vercel and HubSpot[22][31] | Job, customer, automation, integration and AI detail | Unified event format: time, actor/system, action, result, related record. Technical details expand only when useful. |
| **Contextual AI Suggestion** | Close pipeline guidance[26] | Inbox, leads, quotes, invoices | AI proposes one reasoned next action beside the record. It never implies execution; risky actions continue through approval. |
| **Quick Create with Context** | Attio quick actions; Fergus quick actions[23][39] | Global header and customer/job context | `Ctrl/Cmd+K` offers Add customer/lead/job/quote/invoice. Starting from a customer or calendar slot pre-fills only known fields. |
| **Role-native Navigation** | GorillaDesk; ServiceTitan field/office split[5][18] | App shell | Technician sees Today, Jobs, Customers and Inbox first. Office sees Command Centre, Inbox, Schedule and pipeline. Owner/admin gains reports, team, billing and configuration. |
| **Inline Error with Retained Context** | Linear/Vercel operational patterns[28][32] | Every data surface | Preserve the last safe list or page shell, identify the failed region and allow retry. Whole-page errors are reserved for auth/permission loss. |
| **Filter-linked Chart and Table** | Mercury[38] | `/app/analytics` | One shared filter model controls KPI totals, charts and rows, with the active date range always visible. |
| **Offline Draft and Sync State** | ServiceTitan, BigChange and Zuper[5][19][20] | Mobile job execution | Store only explicitly supported field drafts; label Pending sync, Synced or Needs attention. Never claim completion before server confirmation. |

## Patterns to reject

1. A mega-dashboard of equally weighted cards. It hides urgent work and makes an empty workspace look unfinished.
2. A universal dense desktop layout squeezed onto mobile. Field workers need today’s route and close-out actions first.
3. AI chat as a separate destination for every task. Jobrin’s Business Brain should surface memory and suggestions in the record where a decision is made.
4. Decorative motion, glowing gradients and low-contrast glass panels. The target is calm, precise and easy to scan in daylight.
5. Configuration for unimplemented workflows. Coming-soon pages should stay out of daily navigation until there is a usable end-to-end path.
